Privacy by Design

We built this whiteboard differently. Your data never leaves your control.

Your Device, Your Data

Everything is stored in your browser's local storage. Close the tab? Your work is still there when you come back.

No Backend, No Database

Nothing stores your drawings. A tiny relay just forwards bytes between collaborators — and in encrypted rooms those bytes are ciphertext it can never read.

Roles & Sharing

Add a password and share owner, editor, or view-only invite links. View-only is enforced by cryptography — not a setting a viewer can flip. Rotate access anytime.

No Sign-Up

Just click and draw. No accounts, no emails, no tracking. Share a link and start collaborating instantly.

Works Offline

Lost connection? Keep drawing. Your changes sync automatically when you reconnect. Never lose your work.

Real-Time Sync

See everyone's drawings appear instantly. Powered by CRDTs for conflict-free collaboration, even with spotty connections.

Everything you need to draw together

A real whiteboard — not just a shared sketchpad.

Rich Drawing Tools

Freehand pen, a translucent highlighter, and an inline text tool — with adjustable stroke width, colors, and solid/dashed/dotted styles.

Shapes & Arrows

Lines, arrows, rectangles, circles, ellipses, diamonds, and triangles — with optional fills and arrowheads for quick diagrams.

Sticky Notes & Connectors

Drop color-coded sticky notes with wrapped, scrollable text, then link shapes with connectors that stay attached and re-route as you move things around.

Present Live

See everyone's cursor in real time, and grab the laser pointer to draw attention during a walkthrough — its trail fades and is never saved to the board.

Multiple Boards

Organize a room into separate boards. Create, switch, clear, or delete them — everyone stays in sync on whichever board they're viewing.

Export to PNG & PDF

Frame exactly what you want in an interactive preview — pan and zoom — then save a crisp, retina-resolution PNG or a single-page PDF.
